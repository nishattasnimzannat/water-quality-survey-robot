# Hardware Components

This document lists the main hardware components used to build the
Autonomous Multi-Point Water Quality Survey Robot

## Water Quality Sensors

### pH Electrode Sensor Probe and Monitoring Module
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Measures the pH level of the water.

### DS18B20 Compatible Digital Temperature Sensor
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Measures the temperature of the water.

### Water Turbidity Sensor Detection Module
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Measures the turbidity/cloudiness of the water.

## Control and Data Storage

### Arduino Mega 2560 R3
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Acts as the main microcontroller and coordinates
  sensor readings, motor control, and other hardware components.

### MicroSD Card Module
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Provides an interface for storing collected survey data
  on a microSD card.

## Motor and Movement System

### Hobby Gearmotor 6V TT Dual Shaft BO Motor
- **Quantity:** 2
- **Purpose:** Provides mechanical movement to the robot.

### BTS7960 Motor Driver
- **Quantity:** 2
- **Purpose:** Controls the motors and allows the Arduino to regulate
  their operation.

### MG996R Servo Motor
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Provides controlled angular movement for the relevant
  mechanical component of the robot.

## Power System

### 11.7V Battery with Holder
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Provides the primary power source for the robot.

### Hobbywing 3A UBEC 5V
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Provides regulated 5V power for compatible electronic
  components.

### Battery Charger
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Used to recharge the robot's battery.

### BLX-A Cover with Fuse Holder
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Provides a housing and connection point for the fuse.

### Fuse 5A
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Provides overcurrent protection for the electrical
  system.

### 16A 250V Double Rocker Switch SPST ON-OFF
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Acts as the main power switch for the robot.

## User Input

### TTP223 Touch Sensor Module
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Provides touch-based input to the control system.

## Prototyping and Wiring

### Breadboard (Large)
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Used for prototyping and connecting electronic
  components.

### Breadboard (Small)
- **Quantity:** 1
- **Purpose:** Used for additional circuit connections and prototyping.

### Jumper Wires
- **Quantity:** Approximately 100
- **Purpose:** Used to establish electrical connections between
  components.

### Resistors
- **Quantity:** As required
- **Purpose:** Used for electrical signal conditioning and circuit
  connections.
